Privacy & consent

TrainSync is built on one rule: you decide what you share, and every feature still works with less. This page explains in plain language what we collect, why, who can see it, and how to change your mind. It follows the principles of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023: consent for a stated purpose, collecting only what's needed, and your rights to access, correct and erase your data.

What we ask for, and why

Your account needs only a name, email and password. Everything else is optional. Each category has its own switch, which you can turn on or off at any time in Privacy & data. We record every change along with the policy version you agreed to.

Who sees what

  • Before you both opt in: others see your first name, a compatibility score, your goals and an approximate distance (for example "~2 km"). They never see your email, phone number, exact location, measurements or health answers.
  • After you both opt in: your match sees your contact details so you can arrange a session. A trainer sees only the plan targets you choose to share with them.
  • Models: ranking and planning read only the categories you've consented to. The numbers in your plans come from documented formulas and optimisation, never from a chatbot.
  • We don't sell your data, and we don't use advertising trackers.

Your rights

  • See and change your consent at any time. Withdrawing consent stops any further use straight away.
  • Download everything we hold about you as a JSON file, from Privacy & data.
  • Correct your profile at any time.
  • Delete your account. This permanently erases your profile, measurements, plans, logs, matches and session requests. We keep only a minimal record that you withdrew consent (your account id, the dates and the policy version), so we can show we respected your choice.
  • For questions or complaints, contact us. We aim to reply within 7 days.

Safety & reporting

  • You can block anyone. They disappear from your matches and can't contact you.
  • You can report a profile or someone's behaviour. A person reviews every report.
  • For a first session, meet your trainer at a gym or another public place. The certifications on a trainer's profile are what the trainer entered, so use your own judgement too.

Cookies & third-party services

  • One cookie keeps you logged in (trainsync.sid, httpOnly). There are no analytics or advertising cookies.
  • Maps load image tiles from OpenStreetMap's servers, which see your IP address, as any website would. Gym listings come from OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL).
  • "Directions" links open Google Maps in a new tab. Nothing is sent to Google unless you click one.
  • Exercise data comes from free-exercise-db (public domain), nutrient data from USDA FoodData Central (CC0), and personality items from the IPIP (public domain).
