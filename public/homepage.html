How it works

Five steps from sign-up to your first session, and you control what you share at every one.

  1. 01

    Create your profile

    Sign up as a trainee or a trainer, then choose exactly what you share. Body metrics, health, personality, interests and location each have their own switch.

  2. 02

    Know your body

    From height, weight, waist and neck we estimate body fat, lean mass and daily energy needs. Every number is labelled as the estimate it is.

  3. 03

    Get your plans

    A periodised training block and a 7-day Indian meal plan that hit your calorie, protein and fibre targets, with the reasoning shown for every choice.

  4. 04

    Browse ranked trainers

    Every trainer card shows a compatibility score and the top reasons you fit: goals, schedule, coaching style, distance and budget.

  5. 05

    Both say yes

    You request a session and the trainer accepts. Contact details unlock only after both of you opt in, and you can block or report anyone at any time.
